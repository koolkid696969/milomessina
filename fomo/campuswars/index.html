BEFORE YOU’RE IN

Quick answers.

What exactly is Campus Wars?+

A semester-long trading competition on fomo. Your Greek chapter is your team. Chapters compete at their school and nationally, ranked on raw PnL—profit and loss in dollars, not trading activity.

How do I join a chapter that’s already here?+

Ask your chapter lead for your chapter’s invite link. Joining through that link counts toward the roster’s 80% target. The row above shows a registration snapshot, not live onboarding updates.

Who gets the $500—and the extra $250?+

The $500 goes to your qualifying chapter’s top trader at semester end. Your chapter must onboard 80%+ of active members and keep fomo downloaded through the semester. The first fraternity and first sorority at each campus to reach 80% earn an extra $250. Registering alone does not claim that bonus.

Can sororities compete too?+

Yes. Recognized Greek chapters can enter, including fraternities and sororities. Members competing must be 18 or older.

When does it end?+

The listed Fall 2026 season runs August 24–December 11. Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Competition prizes are not guaranteed trading returns.
