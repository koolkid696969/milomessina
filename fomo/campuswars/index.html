What makes my house grow?+

Your chapter’s onboarded member count. Below 15, your lot is under construction. At 15, a finished house appears. As more members join, it gets bigger and taller. The people on each chapter lawn represent its onboarded members. The village checks chapter registrations every 30 seconds and grows as members join.

How does the village connect to Greek Wars?+

Greek Wars is a semester-long trading competition on fomo. Your Greek chapter is your team, and its house in the village shows its onboarding progress. Chapters compete at their school and nationally, ranked on raw PnL—profit and loss in dollars, not trading activity.

How do I join a chapter that’s already here?+

Ask your chapter lead for your chapter’s invite link. Joining through that link counts toward the roster’s 80% target. Your house and the row’s standings update automatically as registrations arrive.

Who gets the $500—and the extra $250?+

The $500 goes to your qualifying chapter’s top trader at semester end. Your chapter must onboard 80%+ of active members and keep fomo downloaded through the semester. The first fraternity and first sorority at each campus to reach 80% earn an extra $250. Registering alone does not claim that bonus.

Can sororities compete too?+

Yes. Recognized Greek chapters can enter, including fraternities and sororities. Members competing must be 18 or older.

When does it end?+

The listed Fall 2026 season runs August 24–December 11. Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Competition prizes are not guaranteed trading returns.
