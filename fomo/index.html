06 — what you'd post

you'll never
start from blank.

Mostly the Content seat's problem, but anyone on the team can post. These are formats working on finance TikTok right now, rewritten for a campus audience. Each comes with the hook to open on — that first line is most of the job.

street interview · wheel

answer the question or show your balance

“spin it. answer the question, or show us the account.”

The format running campus TikTok right now. A spinning wheel of money questions, and the forfeit is showing your banking app. Most people answer, a few show, and the ones who do both are the clip. Bring a cheap wheel and a tripod.

40 min · 6–8 clipsproven format
street interview

how much do you think i make

“guess my salary. you get one try.”

Ask people to guess the earnings of the person standing next to them, then reveal. The gap between guess and truth is the whole video, and the comments argue about it for days.

30 min · 5 clipseasiest start
green screen · reactive

react to the chart, not the news

“this happened four hours ago and nobody on this campus knows.”

Green screen yourself in front of the actual chart or headline and talk over it. It is the highest-trust format in finance because you are showing your source while still being on camera. Speed beats depth here — post inside a few hours.

20 min · rides the newsneeds speed
green screen · faceless option

the tuition chart

“your tuition versus the s&p, same twenty years.”

Your school’s tuition over 20 years against the S&P, nothing else on screen. It writes its own outrage and ends up in group chats. Works fully faceless if you are not ready to be on camera yet.

15 min · shares beat viewsno face needed
running series · highest reach

$100 to $1,000 — follow along

“day 6 of turning $100 into $1,000. we are down $14.”

The single highest-reach hook in finance content, because the hook is not a tip, it is a shared journey. People come back for the number, not the advice. Post the losing days — those outperform the wins.

10 min/day · builds habitbest for growth
running series · monthly

the campus price index

“the burrito is up 9% since september. here is the rest.”

Track five things students actually buy — dining plan, parking pass, that one burrito — against inflation. Repeat monthly until it becomes your signature and people start sending you price changes.

1 hr/month · becomes a franchisehigh repeat value
confession · highest engagement

i lost money and here is exactly how

“i lost $400 in one afternoon. here is the screenshot.”

The confession hook gets roughly double the like-to-view ratio of a tips video. Post the number, post the screenshot, post what you would do differently. It is the fastest trust you can build on a finance account.

Ten minutes with a finance or econ professor. Instant credibility, and it makes your channel look like a publication rather than an account. Most will say yes to ten minutes.

07 — the playbook

how the good
ones do it.

Reverse-engineered from the ambassadors currently doing the best numbers. None of it requires you to be good on camera on day one.

your first week

A realistic seven days. Ship all of it and you'll be ahead of most people who start the same month as you.

day 1

Claim your handle on three platforms. Same name everywhere. Write a six-word bio.

day 2

Record your first video.

day 3

Post #1. Open the group chat. Personally invite 30 people — by DM, not by story.

day 4

Post #2. Reply to every comment within an hour. That matters more than the edit.

day 5

Put your referral link in your bio and pin it in the chat. First signups land here.

day 6

Post #3, and share it in a couple of campus group chats or class threads to reach people outside your first invites.

day 7

Check your analytics in the hub. Kill your worst format, double down on your best.

"Finance content" isn't a channel. "The money desk for Ohio State" is. Your beat is where markets meet one specific campus, and ideally nothing you post could have been posted by someone at another school.

  • Name it like a publication, not a username
  • Lead with a number, a stake or a named place — never "hey guys, so today"
  • If a national account could post it, rewrite it

Consistency beats brilliance. Five decent posts a week outperforms one perfect post a month, because the algorithm rewards frequency and your campus rewards familiarity.

  • Batch it: one shoot day, five outputs
  • Same slots every week — people learn your schedule
  • Spend 45 minutes in the comments after posting
  • Repost your best performer to the other platforms

Views earn money. Signups win campuses. The bridge between them is always a reason to act right now: a tournament, a challenge, a walkthrough you're running in the chat tonight.

  • One clear ask per week, not per post
  • Walk people through their first trade personally
  • Your referral link goes everywhere, every time
  • Hand your top posts to Growth and let them decide what to keep making
